The Briefing

What the public believes versus what the record actually shows.

A knowledge gap is the space between verifiable evidence and inherited belief. Conspiracy theories thrive in these gaps when the event is consequential, the official record has documented holes, and a coherent motive exists for hidden actors. The Shastri–Bhabha case satisfies all three conditions.

01 / The event

Two deaths in Cold War context

Prime Minister Shastri died of an alleged heart attack hours after signing the Tashkent Declaration with Pakistan. Thirteen days later, Air India Flight 101 carrying physicist Homi Bhabha crashed into Mont Blanc, killing all 117 aboard. Both men were central to India's emerging nuclear posture.

02 / The claim

The CIA killed both, in coordination

The explicit allegation of a single CIA operation traces almost entirely to one source: Gregory Douglas's 2013 book "Conversations with the Crow," which claims to transcribe phone calls with former CIA officer Robert Crowley before his 2000 death.

03 / The investigation

NLP as forensic instrument

This project applies named-entity recognition, sentiment analysis, hedging detection, and citation network mapping to demonstrate how a discredited primary source propagates through credible secondary sources via citation laundering — producing a misleading appearance of consensus.

Methodology

Five-stage forensic NLP pipeline.

Each document passes through five layers of computational scrutiny before contributing to the final knowledge-gap analysis.

STAGE 01

Corpus assembly

Thirty-one sources gathered across six tiers, from encyclopaedic to conspiracy-leaning, with metadata on credibility and stance.

STAGE 02

Entity extraction

Named-entity recognition surfaces persons, organisations, and events. A co-occurrence graph maps how entities cluster across the corpus.

STAGE 03

Sentiment scoring

Each sentence containing target entities is scored for sentiment polarity, allowing comparison across publication categories and time periods.

STAGE 04

Hedging detection

A classifier distinguishes factive ("the CIA killed Bhabha") from hedged ("it has been alleged that...") claims, exposing rhetorical drift.

STAGE 05

Citation network

Hyperlinks and citation lists are parsed into a directed graph, exposing Douglas (2013) as a root node behind disproportionate downstream traffic.

The Disaggregation

Two columns. Both matter equally.

Honest analysis requires holding two findings simultaneously: the genuine evidentiary gaps that fuel suspicion, and the documented unreliability of the source that ties them together.

SUBSTANTIATED

Genuine evidence gaps

  • 01No autopsy was conducted on Shastri despite his sudden death abroad.
  • 02The PMO holds one classified file on Shastri's death, refused declassification under RTI.
  • 03Dr. R.N. Chugh, his personal physician, later died in a road accident.
  • 04The Raj Narain inquiry committee report has gone missing from Parliament's library.
  • 05Bhabha's flight data recorder was never made fully public.
  • 06Vikram Sarabhai (1971) — Bhabha's successor — also died without autopsy.
DISCREDITED

Source credibility issues

  • 01"Gregory Douglas" is a pseudonym for Peter Stahl, reported convicted counterfeiter.
  • 02Historian David Irving identified earlier work as "clumsy forgeries" of wartime documents.
  • 03Joseph Trento, who personally knew Crowley: "Douglas is a complete fake and liar."
  • 04Crowley was reportedly suffering Alzheimer's during the alleged interview period.
  • 05French BEA inquiry found CFIT due to a known unserviceable VOR — pilot error.
  • 06An identical AI Constellation crashed at the same Mont Blanc spot in 1950.

Implications

What this case teaches beyond the case.

Beyond the historical question, the corpus reveals patterns that matter for any society reading itself online.

01

Citation laundering is computable

NLP can map the citation graph of any contested claim, exposing how a single low-credibility root propagates through credentialed-looking secondary sources until the original is no longer visible.

02

Hedging is its own claim

"Allegedly" and "reportedly" feel like neutral attribution, but in aggregate they construct a fact-shaped object. Hedging detection lets us measure this drift quantitatively across publications.

03

Gaps and conspiracies are not the same

The honest reading rejects both naive dismissal and unfounded acceptance. Real evidentiary gaps deserve real investigative response — not narrative substitutes invented to fill them.

04

Strategic motive is not evidence

Counterfactual reasoning ("they would have wanted to") feels like proof but is not. NLP reveals how often motive language substitutes for documentary support across the corpus.

05

Public records remain consequential

The single largest variable in the persistence of this theory is the still-classified PMO file. Transparency policy is not separate from misinformation policy — it is upstream of it.

06

Cinema acts as citation

The 2019 film "The Tashkent Files" introduced the conspiracy to a generation that had never read Douglas. Cultural artefacts carry epistemic weight that NLP must learn to read.

Final Dispatch

Are the deaths related?

They are related by political context but not by any documented operational link. Both men were central to India's nuclear posture in early 1966. Their deaths within thirteen days, against a backdrop of Cold War nuclear politics, constitute a striking historical coincidence with a coherent strategic motive — but coincidence and motive are not evidence of execution. — Project conclusion
TAKE 01

The genuine investigative gaps in both cases — particularly the absent autopsy and the still-classified PMO file — leave room for legitimate doubt that the official record is complete.

TAKE 02

The specific CIA-coordination claim rests almost entirely on a single source whose credibility has been comprehensively challenged by historians, journalists who personally knew the alleged informant, and independent researchers.

TAKE 03

The right epistemic stance is neither dismissal nor endorsement, but careful disaggregation — and that disaggregation is precisely what NLP techniques can do at scale.

TAKE 04

Future scope: extend the citation graph to include vernacular Indian-language sources, model how the 2019 film acts as a citation amplifier, and build a real-time misinformation index for similar contested-history cases.
